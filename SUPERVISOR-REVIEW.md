## Accuracy checklist (content & claims)

Tick when confirmed:

- [ ] Company legal name and registration note in footer are correct  
- [ ] Product names and descriptions match approved marketing / catalogues  
- [ ] Fire / FM / TÜV / ISO / SIRIM / Bomba claims match current certificates  
- [ ] Spec tables and numbers match the official catalogue (or are clearly marked “on request”)  
- [ ] RockWool “certificate pending” wording is acceptable for public view  
- [ ] Photos are company-owned or licensed for website use  
- [ ] Catalogue page images (e.g. Certified Quality) may be shown publicly  
- [ ] No competitor or confidential project details appear
